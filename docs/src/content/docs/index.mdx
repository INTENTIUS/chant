## The lexicons

Each lexicon is a plugin for one platform: its types, its lint rules and its output format. Install one and `chant init` scaffolds a project for it. [Lexicons](/chant/lexicons/overview/) covers how they work, and [Extend chant](/chant/lexicon-authoring/overview/) covers writing your own.

<Landing part="lexicons" />

## Already on Terraform, OpenTofu or Pulumi

Keep your roots and programs. The [terraform](/chant/lexicons/terraform/) and [pulumi](/chant/lexicons/pulumi/) lexicons read them as they are. [choudoufu](/chant/lexicons/terraform/ops/#choudoufu) is an OpenTofu fork that runs your roots with ownership markers on the resources. [plunge](/chant/lexicons/pulumi/plunge/) is a Pulumi fork that does the same for Pulumi programs. [Works with](/chant/concepts/comparison/) has the details.

## The lifecycle you choose

The build is deterministic. After it, you choose per environment: observe drift, reconcile the source from the live system, or apply the source to it. [Lifecycle Models](/chant/concepts/lifecycle-models/) compares the three.
