---
title: What chant's pipelines do
description: The path of a change through its plan note, its approval and its release.
diataxis: explanation
---

| Step | What happens | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Change | You push a branch. chant plans the components it touches and the components that depend on them. | [Your first change](/chant/ci/first-change/) |
| Plan note | One note on the change lists the plan for each component. | [Changes and approvals](/chant/ci/changes-and-approvals/) |
| Approval | A person approves the digest of that exact plan. | [Approve a change](/chant/ci/approve-a-change/) |
| Merge | The merge records the plan and deploys nothing. | [Merges and releases](/chant/ci/merges-and-releases/) |
| Release | A person cuts a release from a green commit and promotes it through each environment, behind a gate. | [Merges and releases](/chant/ci/merges-and-releases/) |
| Drift | chant compares what you declared with what is running. | [Drift](/chant/ci/drift/) |

chant writes the pipeline into your repository in [your forge's own format](/chant/ci/your-forge/). A change that needs more than one step runs as [waves](/chant/ci/waves/) or as an [Op](/chant/ci/run-an-op/).

Every step here has a full reference in the sidebar, starting with [the generated pipeline](/chant/guide/pull-request-ci/).
