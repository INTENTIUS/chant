question: "Which record kinds does the reference workspace's design member declare so that studio's contracts, check evidence, drivers, proposals and design sessions are read and written through chant, under which shapes, and how does a session judge records that are not decisions?"
options:
  - id: "a"
    label: "chant's own contract, evidence and driver kinds in the design member, one Markdown session kind whose subjects are several kinds, and proposals as proposed records of existing kinds"
    how: "design/contracts/contract.kind.mjs: C-NNN Markdown records with state draft, approved or retired, approval ranks draft 0, approved 1, retired 1, retired closed and sealed in closed_digest, criteria [{id, text}] in the front matter, the check files pinned by hash in checks (the kind's pins field), reviews with approved_by as the decider, a bare-id or list supersedes, tier and references. design/evidence/evidence.kind.mjs: a JSON kind with idFrom sha256, holding the fields studio's factory writes (contract {id, sha}, check_sha, commit, tree, runner, ok, tests, criteria, output, at, structural, source) with work and lease added; chant workspace records new writes such a record as JSON named for the SHA-256 of its bytes, and work evidence --from {path} attaches it to a work item's criterion. design/drivers/driver.kind.mjs: D-NNN Markdown records with title, intent and members, no lifecycle. The session kind takes session.subjects.kinds, a list of kind files, beside subjects.kind, and the reference session names decisions, contracts and drivers; its schema adds agenda digests, evidence and media, attendance joined, verdict roles, at and note, opened_by, closed_by, follow_ups as <kind>:<id>, and x- fields. A decision's constrains accepts contract:<id>. Studio's proposals map onto proposed work items, lessons and decisions, which an agent may already write."
    tradeoff: "Every studio design record reads and writes through the read and write contracts, and a contract approval is a verdict like a decision's, with its digest and its session, so quorum and seals work the same. Studio rewrites its contracts' front matter (status to state, criteria out of the body, approved_sha replaced by the record digest) and its session writer, and its x-studio verdict subjects become records of a subject kind. A driver sign-off has no reviews list, so it lives only in the session's verdicts."
  - id: "b"
    label: "copy studio's design kinds into the reference workspace as they are"
    how: "Studio's JSON sessions with approvals, verdicts and x-studio subjects, its evidence and session schemas with a shared defs.schema.json, contracts read by parsing the body, and drivers and proposals as README conventions."
    tradeoff: "Studio changes nothing. chant's write verbs refuse JSON sessions, so studio keeps writing record files itself, against ws-074; approvals stay a list chant does not read, under x-studio; and contracts and drivers still have no kind, so the factory keeps parsing them (#3145)."
  - id: "c"
    label: "a session judges decisions only; contract and driver approvals are records of their own"
    how: "An approval kind, one record per approval of a contract, driver or gate, with its digest and roles, and the session lists the approvals it holds."
    tradeoff: "The session kind's contract stays as it is. Every approval becomes a file, a second place holds what a review entry on the contract already says, and a quorum over approvals needs a reader of its own instead of the one records computes for reviews."
  - id: "d"
    label: "as a, with a proposal kind of its own"
    how: "design/proposals/proposal.kind.mjs: free-form notes with a title and constrains, which an agent writes and a person turns into a decision."
    tradeoff: "Studio's proposals keep their shape. A fifth kind holds what the proposed state of work, lesson and decision records already holds, with a proposer, a source and a person who keeps or drops it, and an agent's write scope needs another entry."
  - id: "e"
    label: "as a, with evidence written by the runner that ran the check"
    how: "The factory writes design/evidence/<sha>.json itself and reads it back through chant, as studio does today; chant only reads the kind."
    tradeoff: "No change to records new. A writer that writes a record file itself is what ws-074 and the writer conformance suite (#3159) rule out, and every runner repeats the naming and canonical bytes."
choice:
  option: "a"
  reason: "#3145 asks for a spec that describes a studio repository without studio, and ws-074 for every fact written through chant. Kinds of chant's own put contracts, drivers and check evidence in the read contract, and the write verbs reach all of them once records new writes a content-addressed record: the record is the fields' bytes, named for their hash, so nothing about it can change later and amend, review and close stay refused. The contract follows the decision kind's model rather than a new one: approval ranks keep an approved contract's substance fixed while its state, check pins and reviews move, a change is a new contract that supersedes it, as studio already does, and a verdict on it carries the digest of the text it judged, the same digest the evidence names, so studio's approved_sha needs no field of its own. A session that judges several kinds is a list of kind files in the session declaration, read and checked the way the one kind is, so the one session kind holds studio's design sessions and the decision walks alike; its verdicts name records of any subject kind, which retires x-studio subjects. Criteria move into the front matter so a reader never parses the body, and their ids are what a check's tests, an evidence record and a work item's acceptance all name. Proposals are already modelled: work, lessons and decisions open proposed with a proposer, and an agent's write scope allows them, so a separate kind would only duplicate that."
rejected:
  - option: "b"
    why: "It leaves studio writing record files itself and keeps approvals and contracts outside the read contract, which is what #3145 and ws-074 set out to end."
  - option: "c"
    why: "A review entry on the contract, with its session, already records an approval, and records computes its quorum; a separate approval record would be a second copy with a reader of its own."
  - option: "d"
    why: "The proposed state of the existing kinds already holds an agent's proposal with its proposer and source, and a person keeps or drops it there."
  - option: "e"
    why: "ws-074 rules out a tool writing a record file itself, and records new can write a content-addressed record with no change to its contract."
